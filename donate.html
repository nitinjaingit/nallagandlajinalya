Mandir Nirman

With the divine blessings of Bhagwan Mahavir Swami and the unwavering support of our Jain community, our Jain Jinalaya in Nallagandla is currently operating from a rented premises and serving the spiritual needs of devotees through daily darshan, puja, pratikraman, and other religious activities.

✨ Today, we have a golden opportunity to transform this humble beginning into a lasting legacy by purchasing land and establishing a permanent Jain Jinalaya for present and future generations.

🏛️ A Jinalaya is more than a place of worship. It is a center of faith, values, culture, and spiritual growth that inspires generations to come.

🤝 We humbly seek your generous support for this sacred cause. Every contribution, whether small or large, is a valuable step toward realizing this collective dream of our Sangh.

💎 "Your contribution today will help build a timeless legacy of faith, devotion, and Jain values for generations to come."

🙏 We request you to participate in this auspicious opportunity, contribute generously, and share this appeal with your family, friends, and well-wishers.

🌿 Let us come together to create a beautiful abode of Bhagwan Mahavir in Nallagandla.
